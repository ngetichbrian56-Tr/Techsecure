OVERVIEW

Careful recovery, honest advice.

Data can be lost through accidental deletion, hardware failure, corruption or malware. Acting quickly and carefully gives the best chance of getting it back.

We assess the situation first and tell you plainly what is likely to be recoverable before any work begins.

  • Accidental deletionFiles, folders or partitions removed by mistake.
  • Hardware failureDrives and storage devices that stop working or become unreadable.
  • Corrupted systemsData affected by file system errors or failed updates.
  • Post-incident recoveryRestoring information after a malware or security incident.

QUESTIONS

Common questions answered.

What should I do right after losing data?

Stop using the device straight away. Continued use can overwrite the data you want back. Do not install recovery software on the same drive, and contact us.

Can you guarantee recovery?

No. Success depends on the cause and the condition of the storage. We will give you an honest assessment before starting.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. We treat your information as confidential and only access what is needed to carry out the recovery.

How can I avoid this in future?

Regular, tested backups are the best protection. See our Cloud Backup service.
