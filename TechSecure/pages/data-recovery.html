OVERVIEW

Lost data doesn't have to stay lost.

Data loss usually follows a routine event: a file is deleted by mistake, a drive fails, a system crashes or storage becomes corrupted. The right response depends on what happened and how the device has been used since.

We start by understanding the incident, then recommend the safest recovery approach. We will tell you honestly what is likely to be recoverable before you commit to anything.

  • Accidental deletionRestore files removed from computers, external drives and shared storage.
  • Hardware failureRecover information from drives that are failing, unresponsive or damaged.
  • Corrupted or formatted drivesWork on storage that no longer opens, mounts or reads correctly.
  • Recovery adviceClear guidance on what to do, and what to avoid, while your data is at risk.

QUESTIONS

Common questions answered.

Should I keep using the affected device?

No. Continued use can overwrite data that is still recoverable. Switch the device off, avoid installing recovery software on the same drive, and contact us.

Is recovery guaranteed?

No service can honestly promise that. Results depend on the condition of the storage and what has happened to it since the loss. We will explain the likely outcome before we proceed.

Is my data kept confidential?

We treat everything we handle as confidential and use it only to carry out your recovery. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information.

How long does recovery take?

It varies with the problem, the size of the storage and its condition. We will give you a realistic estimate once we have assessed the situation.
