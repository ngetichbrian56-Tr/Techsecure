QUESTIONS

Common questions answered.

We are a small organization. Do we really need cybersecurity?

Yes. Attackers often target smaller organizations because they assume protections are weaker. Basic measures such as strong passwords, multi-factor authentication and regular updates prevent many common attacks.

Where should we start?

Usually with an assessment of your accounts, devices and data, so effort goes to the biggest risks first. We will recommend priorities rather than a long list.

Can you guarantee we will not be attacked?

No one can. Security reduces the chance and impact of an incident. We will be honest about what a measure does and does not cover.

What should we do if we think we have been hacked?

Disconnect the affected device from the network, avoid deleting anything and change passwords from a clean device. Then contact us so we can advise on next steps.
