Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

TechSecure Solutions ("we", "us") is a technology and cybersecurity company based in Nairobi, Kenya. You can reach us at info@techsecure.co.ke or on +254 746 123 456.

Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us, for example when you send a message through our contact form or contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp. This may include your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in and the content of your message.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, provide the services you request and communicate with you about them. We do not sell your personal information.

Contact form processing

Messages sent through the contact form are received through Netlify, the platform that hosts this website. Netlify processes form submissions on our behalf.

WhatsApp

If you contact us on WhatsApp, your message is handled through WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing your information

We share your information only with service providers who help us operate our website and business, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond and to maintain a reasonable record of our communications, then delete it.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at info@techsecure.co.ke and we will respond.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
