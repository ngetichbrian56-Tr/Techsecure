Privacy Policy.

How TechSecure Solutions collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

TechSecure Solutions (“we”, “us”) is based in Nairobi, Kenya, and provides cybersecurity, data recovery, cloud backup and data analytics services. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website and through our direct communications with you.

You can reach us at info@techsecure.co.ke or on +254 746 123 456.

2. What personal data we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and deliver our services:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (if provided), organization, the service you are interested in and the message you write.
  • Email: your email address, name and the content of messages you send us.
  • WhatsApp and phone: your phone number, name as shown on your account and the content of the messages or calls you have with us.
  • Service delivery: information you or your organization share so we can carry out work, which may include business data or, in some cases, personal data of your staff or customers.

We do not ask you to send sensitive information, such as passwords, through the contact form, email or WhatsApp.

3. How and why we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotes or advice.
  • To provide the services you request and communicate with you about them.
  • To keep records, manage our business and meet legal obligations.
  • To protect our website and systems against misuse, including spam.

We rely on your consent, on the need to take steps at your request before entering a contract or to perform one, on our legitimate interests in running our business, and on legal obligations, as applicable. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where needed and only with service providers that help us operate: Netlify, which hosts this website and processes messages sent through our contact form; our email provider; and WhatsApp (operated by Meta). Messages sent over WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy. We may also disclose data where the law requires it. We do not share your data for third-party marketing.

5. Cookies, fonts and analytics

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives technical information such as your IP address. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers may store or process data outside Kenya. Where this happens, we take steps to make sure your data is protected in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including to respond to you, deliver services, meet legal or accounting requirements and resolve disputes. Enquiries that do not lead to a service are kept for up to 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as the relationship lasts and for the period required by Kenyan tax and accounting law. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

8. How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including access controls and limiting access to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be informed of how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • object to the processing of your data;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or misleading data;
  • ask us to delete data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting earlier processing.

To use any of these rights, email info@techsecure.co.ke. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

10. Children

Our services are for organizations and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through this website.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya at odpc.go.ke.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
